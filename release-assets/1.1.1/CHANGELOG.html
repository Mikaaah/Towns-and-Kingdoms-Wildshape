✦ WILDSHAPE — 1.1.1 ✦

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · Java 21

Dungeon placement

  • Removed Wildshape’s dungeon spacing, density, and overlap filters. Structure mods use their own placement rules again.
  • Removed structure-set overrides from the Wildshape preset editor. The terrain checks for cliffside, flooded, and unsupported starts still apply where configured.

Existing worlds

  • Older Wildshape presets load with their terrain settings, and their retired structure-set overrides are ignored automatically.
  • Separate server datapacks can still set their own structure rules.

Replace your Wildshape JAR and keep your current preset. The placement change applies to newly generated chunks.
