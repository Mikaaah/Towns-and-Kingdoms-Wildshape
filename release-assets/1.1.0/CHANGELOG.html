✦ WILDSHAPE — 1.1.0 ✦

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · Java 21

Dungeons and structures

  • Increased placement opportunities by roughly 25% across 30 selected Overworld structure sets. The final count varies by seed, terrain, and biome.
  • Added foundation checks for Iron’s Spells ’n Spellbooks Catacombs near giant caverns to prevent suspended terrain and structure fragments.

Rivers, islands, and caverns

  • Corrected inland river biomes where deep riverbeds were being classified as marine habitat. Verified flowing water with Streams Reflowing 2.17.4.
  • Fixed Mushroom Fields biome selection along mushroom island shores and where river terrain crosses the island.
  • Prevented unsupported lush clay pools from forming floating clay and water in giant caverns.

World generation changes apply to newly generated chunks.
