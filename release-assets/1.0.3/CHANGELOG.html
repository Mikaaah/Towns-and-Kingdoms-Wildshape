WILDSHAPE — 1.0.3

New adventures above familiar landscapes.

Minecraft 1.21.1 · NeoForge · Java 21

SkyLands compatibility

  • Added optional support for SkyLands NeoForge Port 0.9.4.
  • Adjusted Wildshape’s Overworld build space to accommodate SkyLands’ islands, vegetation and structures through Y=527, while retaining higher configured ceilings.
  • SkyLands continues to control its own sky biomes, island generation, structure placement, creature spawning and configuration.
  • The integration activates automatically when SkyLands is installed. SkyLands and its dependencies remain separate, optional installations.

Existing world support

  • Existing Wildshape worlds receive the required height adjustment when loaded, without manually editing their saved preset.
  • Retained existing terrain generation, world depth, river and cave settings, and saved preset data.
  • Other dimension-type resources keep their native settings.

New chunks only: SkyLands islands appear in newly generated chunks. Existing generated and pregenerated chunks do not gain islands; explore beyond the pregenerated area to find them.

Updating

Update both client and server to Wildshape 1.0.3. Replace the previous Wildshape JAR, install SkyLands and its normal dependencies separately, and restart Minecraft or the server.

The wildshape registry identity is unchanged. Worlds created under the former ultraterraforged namespace still require a separate migration or a fresh world.

Includes the earlier world-creation, terrain-cache, cave-fluid and Streams compatibility fixes.

Verification

The NeoForge build, 53 focused regression tests and 25 flow/search checks passed. Dedicated-server testing on NeoForge 21.1.266 covered upgrading a saved 1.0.2 world with SkyLands and the TNK biome stack, native island generation, saving and reloading, and startup without optional mods.

The sampled existing chunk and saved preset stayed unchanged, and a block at Y=527 survived reload. Full graphical-client and original-server seed/preset verification remain outstanding.
