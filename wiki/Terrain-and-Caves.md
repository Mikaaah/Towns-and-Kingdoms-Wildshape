# Terrain and caves

Large interconnected caves, terrain presets and dungeon-aware generation are described in the [README](https://github.com/Mikaaah/Towns-and-Kingdoms-Wildshape). Modpack stability and every structure interaction still require testing with the actual pack. Existing chunks are not regenerated.

Wildshape **1.0.2** removes ordinary buried aquifer sources and clay beds inside new cavern cuts, schedules exposed water/lava updates and limits Streams carving protection to Wildshape's native raised-water volumes. Surface checks use actual solid ground. Surface water, authored cavern lakes, waterlogged blocks, structures and protected cave biomes retain their safeguards.

Nature's Spirit's Woody Highlands deliberately uses brown terracotta bands. Wildshape preserves these native biome materials. The [1.0.2 audit](https://github.com/Mikaaah/Towns-and-Kingdoms-Wildshape/blob/7c136fc44a215e82c56c5f7fcc0bcc2a85d578b6/docs/WILDSHAPE_WORLDGEN_FIX_2026-11-15.md) records the reproduced mechanisms, test evidence and remaining visual checks.
