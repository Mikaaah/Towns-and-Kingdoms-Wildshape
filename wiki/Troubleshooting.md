# Troubleshooting

Check the loader edition, preset and world type; test in a backed-up or disposable world. For river adapter issues, check Streams Reflowing 2.15.2 and remove duplicate standalone compatibility JARs. Include seed, coordinates, preset, dependency versions and logs in reports.
